The posted checking-account balance at the end of a period, or the balance on the last
imported day when later days are not yet verified. bob draws it at read time from bank
journal evidence; it is never written into a note. The Bank Period Charts header may
show it beside a period's [[stats-strip]], separately from the [[bank-badge]] summary.
