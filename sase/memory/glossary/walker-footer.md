One contextual bob-ledger-tools status-bar panel that explains the active bracket walk. Exactly one member shows at a time, chosen by the persisted bracket family (`s` / `w` / `n`) — never by cursor, timer, or remaining work. The three members are the [[morning-gtd-review-footer]] for `s`, the lane footer for `w` (a pure view over walk-membership counts with lane answer hints), and the links footer for `n` (a destination compass over the note's prev/next links). Every panel fills the same slots — glyph + position, current group, detail, hint, remaining groups, meter — wears its walk's hues, and sheds end-first as the bar narrows. A selected walk always paints, including calm clear states; clear is distinct from loading/unavailable (Tasks indexing, Today loading) and from the unset family, where no saved walk shows no panel. Positions are queue membership, never completion progress, and the panel is a read-only projection: it never stamps, moves, or creates tasks. While review commitments are due off-`s`, a quiet tier-tinted dot on the selector's `s` letter marks them. The walker footer is not the `[[ s w n ]]` selector, a landing toast, the [[day-strip]], or a stats strip.
