---
keyword: Task Link
aliases:
  - "task block link"
---

A block link to an Obsidian task. These can be located anywhere but, when they are the only contents of a sub-bullet on a pomodoro, they are treated as logged/planned tasks for that pomodoro. As another special case, transcluded task links that are the only contents of a sub-bullet on another Obsidian task are treated as sub-tasks (i.e. dependencies) of that task.
