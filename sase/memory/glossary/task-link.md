aliases:

---

A block link to an Obsidian task. These can be located anywhere but, when they are the only contents of a sub-bullet on a pomodoro, they are treated as logged/planned tasks for that pomodoro. Task Links on a task's Depends-On line are [[task-dependency-link]]s. LINKS entries are [[task-relation]]s, and LINKED FROM entries are relations or [[task-mention]]s; none is ever a Pomodoro Task Link.
