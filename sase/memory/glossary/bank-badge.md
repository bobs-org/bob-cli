A read-only money summary that follows one [[day-strip]] or [[stats-strip]] and covers exactly that strip's period: a day, ISO week, quarter, ISO year, or all time. bob supplies the counted in/out totals, waiting rows, and coverage from `bob bank report`. The badge shows each counted side only above $0.00, hides when both sides and period waiting are zero, and names partial coverage. Green marks a period where In exceeds Out; neutral marks a period where In is no greater than Out; amber marks waiting rows when no money was counted. Its tooltip includes exact cents, excluded money, largest outflows, waiting, and coverage. Click, Enter, or Space opens the oldest waiting row, the first counted row for the period, or `bank.md`; it never creates a note or writes the vault. It is not a bank card or a Stats metric.
