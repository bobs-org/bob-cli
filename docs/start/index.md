# Get started with Bob

Bob combines a command-line tool, Obsidian plugins, and an optional Mac capture
panel around one Markdown vault. You can try the command-line workflow without
installing Obsidian plugins.

## Choose a path

- [Is Bob for me?](is-bob-for-me.md) — the workflow, the audience, and what
  each surface covers (about 5 minutes).
- [Install Bob](install.md) — checkout, toolchain, install, and verification
  (about 10 minutes).
- [Your first Bob day](first-bob-day.md) — capture two tasks, choose one for a
  Pomodoro, finish it, and inspect the review queue (about 15 minutes).
- [Continue in Obsidian](obsidian.md) — open the demo in Obsidian with Tasks
  and Bob plugins (about 10 minutes).
- [Continue with Mac Capture](mac-capture.md) — the optional menu-bar panel
  on macOS (about 10 minutes).
- [Getting started contract](../getting-started.md) — exact vault and command
  requirements for the complete workflow.

## Continue

The first-day walkthrough uses a synthetic gardening vault. Afterward, open its
copy in Obsidian or add the Mac panel when it fits your setup.
